Audio

This item only works when running on the client. Client

The sea's sounds on the Audio API: AudioPlayers, AudioEmitters in the world and one AudioListener on the camera, all wired through a single low-pass filter that goes dull while the camera is under the surface. No legacy Sound is made anywhere in the Ocean or its addons. 

Ocean.Audio.Play("rbxassetid://123", nil, { Volume = 0.8 })                -- heard everywhere
Ocean.Audio.Play("rbxassetid://123", point, { Volume = 1, Distance = 800 }) -- from a spot in the world

Functions​

SetMuffle​

</>
Audio.SetMuffle(amount: number) → ()

How dull everything the Ocean plays is, 0 (open) to 1 (as if under water). Eased by whoever calls it.

NewPlayer​

</>
Audio.NewPlayer(name: string) → AudioPlayer?

A new AudioPlayer wired into the shared chain (heard everywhere, through the muffle). For loops that manage their own volume; Audio.Play is the one for one-shots.

Measure​

</>
Audio.Measure(id: string) → ()

Plays id silently once, into an AudioAnalyzer only, to learn how loud it gets (the loudest moment of the whole clip), so a later Audio.Play with Normalize can bring it to a known level whatever asset it is. Takes as long as the clip; one voice, nothing audible. Does nothing for one already measured.

Play​

</>
Audio.Play(
id: string,
position: Vector3?,
options: Options?
) → AudioPlayer?

Plays id once and cleans everything up afterwards. With a position the sound comes from that spot in the world (an AudioEmitter, heard by the listener on the camera, louder the closer); without one it is heard everywhere. Returns the player, or nil where audio is not available (a server) or id is blank.
