Balance

The balance model behind a stagger: a small, deterministic simulation in the spirit of NaturalMotion's Euphoria (the ragdoll behaviour in GTA IV / V and Red Dead). It does not know about parts or joints; it turns "you were shoved this hard in this direction" into a stream of numbers the Controller turns into steps, leaning and a decision.

The character has a lurch (horizontal velocity they are being carried by) and a balance from 0 to 1. Balance drains while they are lurching. At irregular intervals they take a catch-up step: a clean step kills part of the lurch and buys some balance back, a stutter is a half step that buys less and comes sooner, and a misstep kicks them sideways and costs balance. When the lurch has died down with balance to spare they have recovered; when balance hits zero they fall.

Everything random comes from a Random seeded by the stagger, so the server and the simulating client agree on what happens without talking.

Disturb​

</>
Balance:Disturb(
amount: number,--

Balance to remove, 0 to 1.

direction: Vector3?--

Direction of the extra lurch; the lurch added is amount * LurchSpeed.

) → ()

Knocks the model again without restarting it: adds lurch in direction (or the current direction) and takes amount off the balance. The controller feeds bumps into this; you can too.

Step​

</>
Balance:Step(
dt: number,
catch: number?--

0 to 1: how much the player is running the stumble out (walk input lined up with the lurch). Earns balance back and bleeds speed.

) → Outcome--

"Staggering" while undecided, then "Recovered" or "Fell" once and for all.

Advances the model by dt seconds. Call it every frame on the simulating side.
