About this pack

A bright cartoon restaurant tycoon kit in ketchup red, mustard yellow and lettuce green, with thick dark-brown outlines, chunky drop shadows and Bangers headings. Four full screens: a HUD (cash counter with income ticker, restaurant level star and rating bar, order queue, menu buttons, Collect button and a notification toast), an Upgrades window with Kitchen, Staff and Decor tabs and upgrade cards with cost, level and maxed states, a Gamepass Shop with 2x Cash and Auto-Collect cards plus cash packs, and a Leaderboard of top tycoons. It comes twice: a static version to drop in StarterGui and edit in Properties, and a Fusion 0.3 version with smooth, bubbly animations (rolling numbers, sliding tabs, a nudge on 'can't afford' cards, windows that ease in) and a UI Labs story for every piece.
