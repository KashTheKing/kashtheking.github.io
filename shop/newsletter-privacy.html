Newsletter privacy note

The KashTheKing Shop newsletter tells you about new asset packs and sales. This is everything it does with your data.

What is stored

  • Your email address and whether it is waiting for confirmation, subscribed or unsubscribed.
  • When you signed up and confirmed, the page you signed up on, and the consent text you agreed to.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address (to stop abuse). Your IP address itself is not kept.

How it is used

Only to send you the newsletter. Nothing is sent until you click the link in the confirmation email. Your address is never sold or shared. Emails are delivered by Resend, from shop@kashtheking.com.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Its page also has Delete my data, which removes your address and its sign-up record completely. You can also write to shop@kashtheking.com.
