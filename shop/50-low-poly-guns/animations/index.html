50 Low Poly Guns: animations

Every gun in the pack is its own rigged model. Pick a gun and play the clips it ships with. Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom.

    What each gun does

    GunFireReload

    The pack has three clips, shared by every gun: Fire, Reload and Demo (three shots, then a reload, looping). There is no separate Cycle clip. The slide, charging handle, pump or bolt cycles back and forward inside Fire and Demo. A gun only moves the parts it has. This page re-draws each gun from the pack's own build data and plays the pack's keyframes at 30 fps, so it is not a recording from Roblox Studio.
