About this pack

A chunky candy simulator UI kit in bubblegum pink, mint and lemon with dark plum outlines and studded brick buttons. Five full screens: a HUD (level badge, coin and gem counters, menu bricks, daily quest, boost timers, candy bag and Sell button), a Candy Shop with tabs and a grid of pet cards, an Egg Hatch prompt with odds and pet storage, a My Pets window (rarities, boosts, Equip and Equip Best), and a 7-day Daily Rewards window. It comes twice: a static version to drop in StarterGui and edit in Properties, and a Fusion 0.3 version with springy animations (bouncing, sinking buttons, rolling counters, shakes, pops, a rocking egg) and a UI Labs story for every screen and component.
