200 Pixel Items

200 items as full 3D voxel models for drops, pickups and inventories: food, drinks and potions, ores and ingots, coins and gems, crafting materials, farming, magic, loot containers, music and misc, tech. 1 voxel = 0.125 stud, items are 1 to 2.5 studs across and sculpted all the way round. One texture, rigged and animated (Idle bob and spin, Pickup).

100 Voxel Village Builds

100 voxel village assets: ten enterable houses, fifteen service buildings (forge, bakery, tavern, town hall, windmill, watermill, lighthouse ...), walls and gates, paths, bridges and street props, crop fields in growth stages, interior furniture and decorations. Scale: 1 unit = 1 stud for a 5-stud character; a full block is 2 studs and the pixel art is 8 px per stud on every asset; doorways are 4 studs wide and 6 tall; everything snaps to the 2-stud block grid. One material, one pixel-art atlas texture, rigged and animated.

100 Voxel Vehicles

100 blocky voxel vehicles in bright flat colours with pixel-art shading: cars, trucks and service vehicles, bikes and carts, trains with rail pieces, boats, aircraft, spacecraft and fantasy rides. True stud scale for a 5 stud character: 1 voxel = 0.5 stud (0.25 stud on the small ride-ons). Every wheel is its own part with a bone on the axle. One texture, rigged and animated.

150 Voxel Tools & Armor

150 voxel tools, weapons and armour pieces in a bright pixel-art style, in ten material tiers that each have their own shapes: Wood, Stone, Copper, Iron, Gold, Crystal, Emerald, Obsidian, Magma and Void. Every tier has a sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, dagger, hammer, spear and bow, plus a helmet, armor, pants and boots that fit a blocky 5 stud character, and there are 20 extras: shields, buckets, torch, lantern, compass, clock, map and more. 1 voxel = 0.1 stud. Every tool has its grip at the same place, so one hold animation fits all. One texture, rigged and animated.

One material, one pixel-art atlas texture, rigged with shared bone names (Root, Float, Body, Head, Jaw, Tail, ArmL/R, LegL/R, LegFL/FR/BL/BR, WingL/R) and shared actions (Idle, Walk, Attack, Fly, Hurt, Hop).
